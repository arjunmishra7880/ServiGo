Chapter 1: Introduction

1.1 Introduction

In contemporary daily living, domestic maintenance and utility services—such as plumbing, electrical repairs, home appliance servicing, carpentry, and deep cleaning—are essential for maintaining household infrastructure and residential living standards. However, the domestic home services market has historically remained unorganized, fragmented, and heavily dependent on informal communication. At the same time, households face considerable challenges in identifying reliable, skilled, and punctual service professionals. In many cases, independent technicians operate without standard pricing or verified credentials, resulting in mistrust and uncertainty between households and technicians. Therefore, there is a distinct need for an organized system through which essential domestic services can be discovered, scheduled, monitored, and evaluated.

The Web Development Project titled “ServiGo: An On-Demand Local Services Marketplace and Discovery Platform” focuses on developing a web-based solution to address this issue. The proposed platform aims to connect consumers seeking domestic repairs with skilled local service providers, tradespeople, and specialized technicians. According to the project specifications, service providers can provide information regarding their trade, services, hourly rates, location, and availability. Customers can identify and book suitable technicians, while platform administrators oversee service standards and provider verification.

The project combines community participation with modern full-stack web engineering to create a more systematic approach to domestic service delivery. Instead of depending entirely on informal recommendations, the proposed platform provides a centralized system for managing service appointments and coordinating participants. Features such as service category navigation, location-based search, notifications, booking status tracking, and provider ratings help improve the overall hiring experience.

1.2 Purpose of the Project

The main purpose of this Web Development Project is to explore how a technology-based marketplace can help organize local domestic services and facilitate transparent, convenient service transactions within communities. The project seeks to provide a structured method for connecting household consumers with verified service professionals.

1

The platform is intended to make the process of booking local technicians more convenient and organized. A service provider can enter relevant information about their skills, pricing, and service coverage, allowing households to identify service options according to their requirements and location. The integrated communication and booking modules further support this coordination by enabling direct coordination of visit schedules, addresses, and service requirements.

1.3 Objectives of the Project

The major objectives of the ServiGo Local Services Marketplace Platform are as follows:

  1. To develop a web-based platform for managing and coordinating local domestic service requests.
  2. To connect households and commercial clients with verified service providers across essential categories such as Plumbing, Electrical, Appliance Repair, Carpentry, and Cleaning.
  3. To provide service professionals with an accessible method of listing their skills, services, and pricing.
  4. To enable customers to search, compare, and book suitable service providers based on specialization, pricing, customer reviews, and geographic location.
  5. To support direct real-time communication between customers and service providers through integrated in-app messaging.
  6. To provide an interactive, location-based map interface for visualizing nearby service professionals and improving service reach.
  7. To maintain complete digital records of service requests and their progress through an automated booking status tracking workflow.
  8. To establish a community-driven reputation and quality assurance mechanism through star ratings and verified customer reviews.
  9. To provide an administrative dashboard for managing users, service categories, provider verification badges, and reports.

1.4 Background Information

Accessing reliable domestic services is not only a matter of personal convenience but also represents household safety, appliance longevity, and domestic resource management. When domestic repairs are delayed or executed by unqualified individuals, households face property damage and excessive expenditures. Community-based service discovery can therefore play an important role in organizing local labor resources and improving service dependability.

1.5 Significance of the Project

The significance of this project lies in combining community engagement with modern web technology to address inefficiencies in the local home services sector. The platform can provide a structured communication channel between household consumers, independent technicians, and administrators. This can make it easier to identify available local skills and coordinate timely domestic repairs.

From a community perspective, the project promotes participation, transparency, and cooperation. Service providers are provided with an organized way to showcase their trades and secure steady work opportunities, while consumers benefit from competitive, transparent pricing and reliable service standards. Such coordination can strengthen community trust and encourage responsible use of local trades.

The project is also significant from an educational perspective because it provides an opportunity to apply full-stack programming and relational database concepts to a real-world community problem. It connects technical learning with social responsibility and community-oriented problem solving.

1.6 Expected Outcomes

The expected outcome of the project is a functional web-based platform that can facilitate the management and coordination of local domestic service requests. The system is expected to provide separate functionalities for customers, service providers, and administrators.

Through the platform, service providers should be able to register, list customized service packages, and manage appointment requests. Customers should be able to explore categorized services, locate nearby technicians via an interactive map, create bookings, and communicate with service providers in real time. Automated booking tracking and notifications should help participants monitor the progress of service appointments across pending, accepted, completed, or cancelled states.

Chapter 3: Methodology

The methodology of the project focuses on understanding the operational challenges of the local domestic services sector and studying how service seekers can be effectively connected with skilled local providers. The project uses observational methods, informal discussions, interviews, and secondary research to understand the existing service procurement process and identify the requirements for the proposed ServiGo Local Services Marketplace Platform.

Observation was used to understand how domestic maintenance requests are generated, scheduled, and resolved in the community. The process of finding technicians and the challenges involved in identifying available workers, price negotiation, and reliability were considered. Attention was given to factors such as the availability of skilled workers, communication between participants, travel logistics, and timely completion of work.

Discussions with people involved in hiring domestic help and independent technicians provided information about how customers currently search for help, how technicians acquire work leads, and how both parties coordinate timing and pricing.

Secondary research was also used to gather information related to the gig-economy, domestic services, platform trust mechanisms, and the use of technology in local market organization. Online articles, reports, research papers, and other relevant sources help provide background information and an understanding of existing approaches to local service discovery.

The main tools used for data collection include observation notes, interview questions, informal discussions, online research, and documentation of relevant information. These methods were selected because the project addresses a real community problem and requires an understanding of the experiences and requirements of different stakeholders. Observation provides practical understanding, interviews and discussions provide direct viewpoints, and secondary research provides supporting background information.

The information collected through these methods helps identify the requirements of the proposed platform. Based on these findings, features such as category-based browsing, location-based map search, notifications, booking tracking, and in-app chat can be included in the system. The proposed platform aims to connect households with verified service providers to make domestic service discovery and delivery more organized and efficient.

Chapter 5: Conclusion and Recommendations

Conclusion:
The project helped the team develop a better understanding of the problem of unorganized domestic services and the importance of structured digital coordination. The community interactions and observations showed that connecting households with verified local technicians can significantly improve convenience and trust. The study also highlighted the importance of coordination between consumers, service providers, and administrators. These findings contributed to understanding the practical requirements of the proposed ServiGo Local Services Marketplace Platform. The platform is designed to connect different stakeholders and provide facilities for service listing, booking tracking, location-based map search, notifications, and reviews.

Key Findings:
The study found that one of the major challenges in local services is the lack of proper communication and price transparency between customers and independent tradespeople. Timely coordination is also important because domestic emergencies (such as plumbing leaks or electrical shorts) require prompt resolution. Digital tools can support the process by providing upfront pricing, verified credentials, and real-time messaging. The findings indicate that a centralized digital platform can make service information more accessible and can improve coordination between all participants.

Recommendations:
Based on the findings, more awareness should be created among independent tradespeople about utilizing digital tools to expand their customer base instead of relying solely on informal word-of-mouth. Households should be encouraged to participate in organized platform bookings with transparent reviews. The proposed platform can be further improved by adding more location-based features, payment gateway integration, automated notification systems, and technician availability calendars. Future development can also focus on verification standards, safety protocols, and community engagement. Regular feedback from customers and service providers can help improve the platform and make it more useful for organizing local service economies.

Appendix A: Stakeholder Fieldwork Notes and Interview Transcripts

As part of the primary requirement analysis for the ServiGo Local Services Marketplace Platform, the project team conducted structured fieldwork across suburban and urban residential clusters in Mumbai. The primary objective was to capture direct insights from two key demographic groups: independent blue-collar service professionals operating in the informal market, and residential household consumers. Below are summarized transcripts of two representative interactions.

Case 1: Primary Interview with Independent Service Technician
Interviewee: Amit Kumar (Self-Employed Plumber, 8 Years Work Experience, Andheri East, Mumbai)
Q1. How do you currently obtain new customer leads, and what operational challenges do you experience?
Response: "I rely almost entirely on neighborhood hardware shops referring my phone number, or past customers calling me directly. Some weeks I receive four to five jobs in a day, while in other weeks I sit idle for two to three days without any work. When I go to new clients, negotiations are always stressful because they argue about labor charges after I finish the repair."
Q2. How would an online platform like ServiGo assist your daily business operations?
Response: "Having a verified profile with my fixed pricing and reviews would eliminate bargaining. A mobile web dashboard showing incoming booking requests with exact customer addresses, dates, and direct chat would help me plan my travel route and avoid wasting time."

Case 2: Primary Interview with Household Consumer
Interviewee: Rahul Sharma (Residential Apartment Owner & IT Professional, Mumbai)
Q1. What major difficulties do you encounter when hiring local domestic technicians?
Response: "Whenever we experience an electrical short-circuit or plumbing leak, finding an available technician quickly is chaotic. Local handymen quote arbitrary prices on the spot, show up hours late, and there is no guarantee of work quality. More importantly, letting completely unknown, unverified strangers into our home creates safety concerns for my family."
Q2. What features would give you the most confidence when booking a technician online?
Response: "Administrative verification badges confirming background checks, transparent hourly and fixed rates, an interactive map showing how close the provider is, and genuine 1 to 5 star ratings from neighbors in my locality."

B.3 Administrator Trust & Verification Protocol

To ensure platform credibility and consumer safety, the administrative module enforces a rigorous four-stage verification protocol before granting the verified provider trust badge:

  1. Identity Verification: Submission and validation of government-issued identity credentials (such as Aadhaar or PAN card) to establish authentic legal identity.
  2. Trade Experience Audit: Verification of vocational trade diplomas, apprenticeships, or formal confirmation of declared years of field experience in plumbing, electrical, or carpentry trades.
  3. Operational Phone Authentication: Direct verification and telephonic confirmation of active contact details to ensure customer reachability during scheduled visits.
  4. Quality Rating Threshold Review: Continuous probationary monitoring requiring newly onboarded providers to maintain an average customer review rating of at least 4.0 out of 5 stars across initial bookings.

Providers failing to meet verification standards remain categorized as unverified, while verified service professionals receive the blue trust badge displayed prominently across category search lists and interactive map markers.
